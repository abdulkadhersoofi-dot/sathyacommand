# Phase 7 · Banquets, sales & guest channels

Weeks 35–42 of the [blueprint](../HMS_BLUEPRINT.md). **Done when** a full wedding runs end to end in the system, from enquiry to final bill.

**Status:** every step is built. The done-when needs a real wedding: once the resort runs on the live HMS, sales take one wedding from enquiry to the final bill in it, with the departments working from its event order, and what they had to do outside the system comes back as fixes before Phase 7 is closed.

## Choices to confirm

- **Each line of an event bill at its own rate**: venue rental, decor and services at 18%, food at the restaurant's rate (5% while no room sells above ₹7,500). CA check: whether a hall-with-food package is instead one composite supply taxed at its principal supply's rate.
- **GST on an advance** is spread over the rates of the accepted quote; before any quote, at 18%. CA check
- **What is kept on a cancellation** is invoiced as a cancellation charge at the rates the advances were taxed. CA check
- **Plates**: billed at the guarantee, or the number served if more; the kitchen cooks 5% over the guarantee.
- **Turnaround**: 3 hours kept free around each booking on every venue until the resort sets its own.
- **Room blocks**: rooms held come off general sale at once and go back at the cut-off (two weeks before arrival unless set); guests in a block pay their own bills, or the company's when the block is for a company account. Wedding hosts who pay for their guests' rooms: to decide with the resort (the rooms could be charged to the event's bill).
- **Contracted rates** are set by the GM; sales proposes them.
- **Website deposit**: the first night with its GST, within two hours, when a gateway is set up; otherwise pay at the resort. The cancellation terms shown to guests are still to be written with the resort.
- **Room service from the room page** is a request, not a kitchen ticket: the kitchen calls the room to confirm and punches it as a room-service order, so nothing is cooked or charged unseen.
- **Guest messages** wait for the desk to send them from its own WhatsApp until a WhatsApp Business provider is chosen; with one, they go by themselves (each message needs its template approved by WhatsApp first).
- **ID at online check-in**: only the type and last four characters are kept, as at the desk; a copy of the ID is not uploaded. If the MD wants copies kept, they need a deletion period (e.g. 30 days after check-out) decided first.
