# Phase 8 · HR & payroll

Weeks 41–46 of the [blueprint](../HMS_BLUEPRINT.md). **Done when** two payroll months run in parallel with the current method and match.

**Status:** every step is built. The done-when needs the resort's real payroll: HR runs two months in the system beside the payroll used today, compares each with **Compare with current payroll**, and puts right whatever differs (a figure, a rule or the data) until both months match to the rupee; then Phase 8 is closed.

The rules (departments, leave, holidays, PF, ESI, professional tax, welfare fund, bonus, gratuity, salary TDS) are in [`server/seed/payroll.yaml`](../../server/seed/payroll.yaml), for HR and the CA to review.

| 8.2 | **Payroll** (People → **Payroll**). | 8.3 | **Income tax on salaries** (People → employee → **Income tax**; Staff → **My pay**). ## Choices to confirm

- **Leave**: casual 12 and sick 12 days a year (not carried forward), earned leave one day a month after 12 months' service with up to 24 carried forward (Tamil Nadu Shops and Establishments Act); sick leave above two days needs a medical certificate. HR check against the resort's standing orders and appointment letters.
- **Holidays**: the four national holidays (26 January, 1 May, 15 August, 2 October) and five festival holidays chosen by HR (Tamil Nadu Industrial Establishments (National, Festival and Special Holidays) Act). Leave does not count holidays; weekly offs come from the roster.
- **Code on Wages**: allowances above half the total pay count as wages. CA check, with the structures the resort uses now.
- **Aadhaar**: only the last four digits are kept, as for guests.
- **Pay for a part month** is by calendar days (a 30-day month pays 1/30 a day); overtime by 26 working days of 8 hours, at twice the wages under the Code on Wages. CA / HR check against the appointment letters.
- **Absence**: a day on the roster with no clock-in, no leave and no holiday is leave without pay unless HR changes it; people without the app or a roster are only docked for approved leave without pay.
- **PF** on wages up to ₹15,000 unless the employee chose full wages; **ESI** coverage fixed for each contribution period; **professional tax** half-yearly on the half's gross, at the local body's slabs (still to confirm); **welfare fund** on those employed on 31 December. CA check
- **Bonus and gratuity** are provided for each month (8.33% of wages up to ₹7,000; 4.81% of wages); paid in 8.3. CA check
- **Salary tax** is worked out from the declaration and projected each month; proofs are checked by HR before the last quarter, and the tax follows the declaration as it stands. Surcharge (income above ₹50 lakh) is not worked out. The Income-tax Act, 2025 renumbers sections (salary TDS was section 192): the files say 192 until the CA confirms the new reference. CA check
- **Pay before the system** for the current year goes in each person's declaration as earlier salary, with the tax deducted on it, so the year's tax is right from the first month.
- **Settlement**: leave encashment on earned leave only; gratuity from the provision account (any shortfall shows there for the CA's year-end entry); the bonus provided this year paid out; the last month's salary through that month's payroll. CA check
- **Yearly bonus for those still employed** is provided for each month (8.2); paying it out at the year's end is to be agreed with the resort (the provision account holds it until then).
- **The MD's approval is the last step** and may come without the GM's check; the yaml's `payroll_release_needs` names both.
