# Data export checklist

Data to move from today's systems into the new HMS. Excel or CSV is fine for every item. Ask the current HMS / POS vendor for a full export if menus don't offer it.

| # | Data | From | Needed for | Received |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Room list with room numbers, types, floors | HMS | Phase 2 set-up and 3D model | ☐ |
| 2 | Rate plans, seasons, packages | HMS / rate sheets | Phase 2 | ☐ |
| 3 | Future reservations (all fields) | HMS | Go-live migration | ☐ |
| 4 | Advances / deposits held against future bookings and events | HMS / accounts | Go-live migration | ☐ |
| 5 | Guest history (name, contact, stays, preferences) — consent status if known | HMS | Guest profiles | ☐ |
| 6 | Company and travel-agent accounts with credit limits and open balances | HMS / Tally | City ledger | ☐ |
| 7 | Restaurant menus with prices, tax category, station | POS | Phase 3 | ☐ |
| 8 | Bar menu: brands, sizes, peg prices | POS | Phase 3 | ☐ |
| 9 | Item master and current stock by store | Stores register | Phase 5 opening stock | ☐ |
| 10 | Vendor master: GSTIN, PAN, bank, MSME status, TDS category | Tally / accounts | Phase 5–6 | ☐ |
| 11 | Chart of accounts and opening balances | Tally | Phase 6 | ☐ |
| 12 | Employee master: designation, department, salary structure, PF/ESI numbers | HR / payroll | Phase 8 | ☐ |
| 13 | Event bookings (future) with packages and advances | Banquet register | Phase 7 migration | ☐ |
| 14 | Last 12 months' monthly occupancy, ADR, F&B revenue | HMS / Tally | Baseline for reports and forecasts | ☐ |
| 15 | Samples of every printed bill, KOT, invoice and register | All departments | Print formats | ☐ |

**Privacy:** guest and employee files contain personal data. Share them only through a private channel (not WhatsApp groups), and only with the project team.
