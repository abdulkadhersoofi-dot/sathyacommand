# Department workshops

One 60–90 minute session per department, with the department head and one experienced staff member.
For every department: **bring a copy of every form, register, report and printout you use today** (photos are fine).

Each session ends with three lists: what works today and must be kept, what wastes time, and what is missing.

## Front office & reservations
1. Walk through a normal day: first arrival to night audit. Where are bookings recorded today?
2. How do bookings arrive (phone, walk-in, website, OTAs, travel agents, corporates)? Share of each?
3. Check-in steps: which IDs are collected, how are they stored, how is Form C done for foreign guests?
4. Deposits and advances: how are they taken and recorded? Refund process?
5. How are bills split between company and guest? How are company credit accounts followed up?
6. Cashier shift handover: how is cash counted and handed over?
7. Night audit: what is checked, what reports are printed, who reads them?
8. Group and wedding room blocks: how are rooming lists handled?
9. Most common guest complaints at the desk?
10. Which reports does the MD or GM ask for most often?

## Restaurant (F&B service)
1. Outlets, opening hours, number of tables and covers on a normal day and a peak day.
2. How do captains take orders today (pad, tablet, POS)? How do orders reach the kitchen?
3. How are cancellations and voids handled and approved?
4. Discounts and complimentary meals: who can give them, how are they recorded?
5. Room service: how are orders taken, delivered and charged to the room?
6. Bill settlement: payment types, split bills, service charge practice.
7. Staff meals: how are they recorded?
8. Feedback collection today?

## Kitchen
1. Stations and the number of cooks per shift.
2. How are KOTs received and tracked? How do you know an order is late?
3. Are there standard recipes with quantities? For which dishes?
4. How are daily vegetables, meat and fish ordered and received?
5. How is wastage recorded?
6. Temperature logs, cleaning schedules, FSSAI inspections: how are they kept?
7. Banquet production: how do you get the menu and numbers for an event?

## Bar
1. Brands stocked, bottle sizes, peg sizes, cocktails.
2. How are TASMAC purchases made, received and recorded?
3. Daily excise stock register: format and who fills it. Bring a copy.
4. How are open bottles counted at closing?
5. Breakage and spillage process.
6. Dry days and licensed hours: how are they enforced today?
7. How is VAT billed and returned?

## Housekeeping & laundry
1. Number of attendants per shift, how rooms are allocated today.
2. Room status communication with the front desk (phone, register, walkie-talkie?).
3. Cleaning standards: checklist per room type? Time per room?
4. Inspection process and who releases rooms.
5. Minibar: items, how consumption is recorded and charged.
6. Linen: par levels, in-house laundry or vendor, how counts are kept, losses.
7. Guest laundry: price list and process.
8. Public areas: who cleans what and when.
9. Lost & found process.

## Stores, purchase & pantry
1. Stores that exist and who controls each.
2. Purchase process: indent, approval, PO, receiving, bill.
3. Vendor list: how many, which are MSME-registered?
4. Stock counts: frequency, method, variances.
5. Perishables: expiry tracking?
6. Issue process to departments.

## Accounts
1. Chart of accounts in Tally (export a copy).
2. How sales from front office, restaurant and bar reach Tally today.
3. GST: who prepares returns, how ITC is reconciled, e-invoicing today?
4. TDS: vendors under TDS, how deduction is decided, who files quarterly statements?
5. VAT on liquor: monthly process.
6. OTA payouts: how commission, TCS and TDS are reconciled.
7. Bank and card reconciliation process.
8. Month-end closing steps and timelines.
9. Reports given to the MD each month.

## HR & payroll
1. Headcount, departments, designations, shift patterns.
2. Attendance method today.
3. Payroll components, PF/ESI coverage, professional tax.
4. Leave policy and holiday list.
5. Staff accommodation and meals.
6. Outsourced staff and agencies.

## Engineering & maintenance
1. Major equipment list (DG sets, transformer, pumps, ACs, STP, pool plant, kitchen equipment).
2. AMCs in place and expiry dates.
3. How faults are reported and tracked today.
4. Meter readings taken (electricity, DG, water, LPG) and how often.
5. Licences handled by engineering (fire NOC, electrical inspection, DG approval, TNPCB).

## Security
1. Gates, guards per shift, patrol routes.
2. Visitor and vehicle register format.
3. CCTV coverage and recorder location.
4. Incident reporting today.
5. Emergency and evacuation procedure.

## Sales & banquets
1. How enquiries arrive and are followed up.
2. Venue booking calendar today (register, Excel, Google Calendar?).
3. Package price sheets and menus (bring copies).
4. Advance schedule and cancellation terms.
5. How the event details reach kitchen, housekeeping, engineering and security.
6. Corporate accounts and contracted rates.
