# Phase 5 · Stores, purchase & inventory

Weeks 25–30 of the [blueprint](../HMS_BLUEPRINT.md). **Done when** a full month closes with stock variance under the agreed limit.

The kitchen, bar, engineering and linen already keep their own stock books (Phases 3 and 4). Phase 5 adds the main store and the purchase cycle around them: goods received or issued into a department store land in that department's book, so nothing is counted twice.

| Step | What | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | **Stores, items and vendors**: the stores (main, kitchen, bar, housekeeping, engineering, linen), each tied to the book it keeps stock in; the **item master**: stock unit and purchase unit with the conversion (1 bag = 25,000 g, 1 case = 48 pcs), HSN, GST rate, input tax credit (claimable, not claimable, blocked), perishable and shelf life, reorder level and quantity, barcode, and the link to the kitchen ingredient, bar brand, spare part or linen item (same unit enforced); one step brings every existing kitchen, bar, spares and linen item in. The **vendor master**: GSTIN checked for typing mistakes (check character), state and PAN taken from it (IGST for vendors outside Tamil Nadu), PAN format and holder type (individual / HUF / company / firm) for the TDS rate, TDS category from the tax table (194C, 194J, 194-I, 194H, 194Q…), MSME status with Udyam number and written agreement (15 or 45 days to pay), payment terms, what they supply; **bank details only by accounts**, masked for everyone else, and a changed account logged separately for God Mode. **Rate contracts** per item and vendor with validity (no overlaps), the best current rate shown on each item | Done · 7 API tests + PAN/IFSC checks + browser run |
| 5.2 | **Indents and purchase orders**: departments (kitchen, housekeeping, bar, engineering, front office) raise numbered indents (IND/26-27/…) in purchase units with an estimate at the best rate; approved or turned down (with a reason) by the GM or operations manager, never by the person who asked; stores turn ticked indents into draft POs, each line going to the vendor with the best current rate (one PO per vendor and store). **Purchase orders** numbered gap-free per financial year (PO/26-27/…), rate from the vendor's contract unless typed, GST line by line (CGST + SGST, or IGST for vendors outside Tamil Nadu), refused for items without a GST rate; submitted POs within the raiser's limit (stores ₹25,000) are approved at once, above it they wait for the GM (up to ₹5 lakh) or the MD; printed PO with HSN, tax split, amount in words and approver, marked DRAFT until approved; sent; cancelled with a reason (indent lines freed). **Market list**: perishables (or everything) below their reorder level, read from the department's own book (kitchen, bar, spares, linen) or the store's ledger, less what is already on order, at the best current rate; ticked lines become draft POs per vendor | Done · 8 API tests + browser run (chef's indent on a phone, approval, ordered, submitted, printed) |
| 5.3 | **Goods received**: against an approved PO (or, by the purchase manager with the reason, a cash purchase without one), each line counted, accepted after the quality check (the rest rejected with the reason), up to 5% over the order, batch and expiry (shelf life fills it in for perishables, expired goods refused), delivery challan and photo; numbered GRN; accepted goods posted at the PO rate into the book the store keeps: the store's own ledger (with batches for first-to-expire), or straight into the kitchen ingredient book (cost updated), bar register, spares book or linen shelf; GST that cannot be claimed is added to the goods' cost; the PO becomes part or fully received; expiring stock in 3 days listed. **Vendor bills**: entered once per vendor bill number, from the receipts they cover (lines filled in) or as service lines (AMC, contract work); CGST + SGST or IGST, input tax credit only where claimable; **three-way match** against the PO's rate and the quantity received and not yet billed, every difference listed; a matching bill is passed by a second person in accounts, a difference or a bill with no PO only by the GM with the reason; due date by the vendor's terms, never past the **MSME deadline** (15 days, or 45 with a written agreement), alerted ten days ahead, and a late payment recorded. **Payments**: TDS worked out by the vendor's category on the value before GST, with the yearly thresholds (e.g. 194C at 1% for an individual, 2% others; 20% without a PAN); above the accounts limit (₹25,000) the GM approves; marked paid with the UTR or cheque number; more than ₹10,000 in cash to one party in a day warned (not deductible); **TDS register** by section and quarter | Done · 10 API tests + browser run (receive with a reject, bill from the receipt, pass, pay with 194C TDS) |
| 5.4 | **Store operations**: what is in each store, in purchase units, at **weighted average cost** and value (department stores read from their own books), below-level and next expiry flagged; **opening stock** once per item; **issues** to a department that uses them up and **transfers** to another store (into the kitchen book at average cost, so food cost follows), refused beyond the stock, numbered ISS/…, counted against the indent they fill (indent then shows Issued); perishables leave **first to expire first**, expired batches written off at cost; **counts** on the phone (search or scan the label's barcode / QR), each line against the book at average cost, the variance corrected at once only within the counter's limit (stores: none) and otherwise after the **GM's approval**, from the book as it stands then; **breakage register** for crockery, cutlery and glassware by department, borne by the house, a member of staff (named) or charged to the guest's bill with GST; printable QR labels per store; reorder suggestions are the market list's “everything below its level” | Done · 9 API tests + browser run (opening stock, issue, count found by barcode, breakage, phone) |
| 5.5 | **Liquor store and excise**: the **stock register** by class, brand and bottle size (opening, received, sold, breakage, count adjustments, closing) **in bottles** and closing in bulk litres, for any day or period up to three months, opening carried from the previous day; printed **day by day** or as the **period statement**, with the FL-3 licence number and signature lines; **TASMAC purchases** now need the transport permit number (and date) and record the **TCS** collected (1%, 206C(1)) as on the invoice, with a TCS credit register by quarter; liquor can no longer be ordered on a purchase order or taken in on a goods receipt: only through a TASMAC purchase; **peg measures and scales** registered with their legal-metrology stamp and due date (a year after stamping unless given), on the compliance calendar with reminders 60, 30 and 7 days ahead, restamping restarts them | Done · 6 API tests + browser run (TASMAC invoice with permit, pegs sold, excise view, printed statement, measure added) |
| 5.6 | **Month close**, store by store: the month valued from the book the store keeps (main and housekeeping ledgers at cost, the kitchen ingredient book, the bar register at each brand's latest TASMAC cost per ml, the spares book, linen owned at the item's last cost): opening, received, issued or used, losses (breakage, expiry, wastage, discards), **count variance** and closing; the **closing count** must fall in the month's last 5 days (a stores count, which now works on any store's book, the kitchen's own count, or an approved bar count), except for a store that held and moved nothing; the variance is a share of what the store gave out in the month (of what it held, if it gave nothing out) and is held against the **limit agreed for that store** (1% until set, changed only by the GM); over the limit the close needs an explanation; the **GM signs off** (a close the GM prepares is signed off by preparing it; sent back, it is redone); months close in order, and a closed month (or one with the GM) is **locked**: no stock movement can be dated in it; the kitchen shows **theoretical against actual food cost** (recipes against wastage and count loss) for the month; printed **month-end stock statement** of every store for accounts and the auditor. Kitchen issues out of the kitchen book now count as consumption, not as count differences | Done · 7 API tests + browser run (October closed: main store 0.14% against 1%, housekeeping over the limit with an explanation, statement printed, phone) |

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Stores | stores, items, vendors (not bank details), rate contracts; indents, purchase orders up to ₹25,000, market list; receiving goods; issues, transfers, counts (any difference to the GM), breakage; preparing the month close |
| Accounts | vendors with bank details; enter and pass bills (not their own); pay vendors up to ₹25,000 (above: GM); TDS register |
| Department heads (chef, F&B, executive housekeeper, bar, chief engineer, front office) | raise indents |
| Operations manager | approve indents |
| GM | approve indents and purchase orders up to ₹5 lakh, count variances, sign off month closes and set each store's variance limit, everything else |
| F&B manager, executive housekeeper, bar manager | record breakage |
| MD | everything, any amount |

## Waiting on

- **CA**: HSN code and GST rate for each item, and which items' input tax credit is blocked (staff food, gifts); TDS category for each service vendor
- **CA / excise**: the register format the Prohibition & Excise department wants (columns, bottles or bulk litres, daily or monthly); TCS base (the invoice value with or without VAT)
- **CA**: GST on damage charged to guests (18%, SAC 999794 assumed)
- **CA**: confirm TDS on the value before GST when GST is shown separately, and deduction at payment (rather than at booking the bill) until the accounts module books bills to ledgers in Phase 6
- **Resort**: peg measures and scales with their last stamping certificates; the FL-3 licence number (Restaurant → Licences & tax)
- **Resort / MD**: the agreed stock variance limit for each store (now 1% of what the store gives out in a month), and the day of the month-end count
- **Resort**: opening stock in each store (a full count on the go-live day); the counting limit for stores staff (now ₹0: every difference goes to the GM)
- **Resort**: the vendor list with GSTIN / PAN, MSME certificates (Udyam) and whether each has a written payment agreement; current rates
